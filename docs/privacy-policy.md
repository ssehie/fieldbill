# Privacy Policy — FieldBill
Effective Date: April 14, 2026

FieldBill respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is used by the app and how it is handled.

## Summary
FieldBill does not require an account and does not store user data on our servers. Most information stays on your device. Some features use third-party services (for example, Google Places) to provide address suggestions.

## Information We Use
FieldBill stores the following data locally on your device:
- Customer names, phone numbers, emails, and addresses
- Job details, labor, parts, and invoice information
- Optional audio notes you record in the app

## Third-Party Services
FieldBill uses these services to provide features:
- Google Places API for address autocomplete. When you type an address, that input is sent to Google to retrieve suggestions.
- Device calendar if you choose to add a job to your calendar.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## Permissions
Depending on how you use the app, FieldBill may request:
- Location (to fill an address from your current location)
- Microphone (to record audio notes)
- Calendar (to create events)

You can deny permissions and still use the app.

## Data Retention
All job and customer data is stored on your device. You can delete the app to remove all stored data.

## Children’s Privacy
FieldBill is not intended for children under 13.
